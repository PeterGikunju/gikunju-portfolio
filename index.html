01   /   About

I like working on the systems behind everyday work.

How a shop handles customer questions. How a landlord tracks rent. How a receipt gets from a database into someone’s hands.

Those are the kinds of details I think about when I build software. My Mathematics and Computer Science studies at Kenya Methodist University shape how I approach data and systems; project work has taught me to make the result clear for the people using it.

Outside software, I enjoy cycling, swimming, and getting out into nature. I’ve also supported community tech workshops through Flickstars.
